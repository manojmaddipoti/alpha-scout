# NVDA — Illustrative Research Memo

> This abbreviated example demonstrates Alpha Scout's output structure. Values
> below are placeholders, not current market data or investment advice.

## Verdict

**WATCHLIST** — high-quality growth and a strong competitive position can still
produce disappointing returns if the entry valuation already discounts the
bull case.

## Scenario framework

| Case | Revenue assumption | Margin assumption | Valuation assumption | Thesis implication |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Bear | Lower growth | Compression | Multiple contracts | Material downside |
| Base | Moderating growth | Stable | Multiple normalizes | Positive, below 2x |
| Bull | Sustained growth | Expansion | Premium persists | 2x becomes plausible |

## Variant perception

The key question is not whether demand is strong; it is whether growth,
competition, supply, and customer economics can remain better than the
expectations already embedded in the valuation.

## Evidence to verify

- Current financial quality and valuation through the market-data tool
- Peer growth, margin, and valuation through the competitor tool
- Management discussion and risk factors from the latest SEC filing
- Recent demand, supply, product, and regulatory developments through web search

## Kill criteria

- Material, sustained loss of share in the core workload
- Cash conversion deteriorates without an offsetting investment payoff
- Customer concentration or export restrictions impair the base case
- Valuation rises while forward fundamentals are revised down
